Engineer Mode

Privacy Policy • Last updated: November 25, 2026

Summary: Engineer Mode has no ads and no purchases, and your progress is stored on your device. Version 1.0 has no internet access and collects nothing. Later versions add two optional features that use the internet: Google Play Games sign-in, and anonymous usage and crash reports. Both are off until you choose them, and you can turn them off at any time.

This policy applies to Engineer Mode: Jetpack Runner (the “App”), published by Vishudh Tech Solutions (“we”, “us”).

1. What we collect

Version 1.0 does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission, so it cannot send information off your device, and it collects nothing. Later versions can send limited information only through the two optional features described in section 3, and only after you opt in. We never sell your information or use it for advertising. In particular we do not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or address.
  • Your Advertising ID, IMEI or MAC address. (The optional features below use a random per-install identifier, not a hardware or advertising identifier.)
  • Location data, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • Usage analytics or crash reports, unless you choose to share them (section 3).

2. What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following in its private app storage (a local Room database and a Preferences DataStore). We cannot see it, and it is never transmitted:

  • Progress: your CPU Core, Memory Chip and Knowledge Chip balances, upgrades, unlocked characters, jetpacks, worlds and cities, missions, score multiplier and achievements.
  • Run history: the score, distance, world, city, character, jetpack and cause of failure of your past runs, and your Daily Outage results. The Daily Outage challenge is chosen from your device’s clock, not from a server.
  • TechPedia: which concepts you have studied or answered.
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration switches, whether you finished the tutorial, and your chosen touch-control area.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave the device even through your Google account. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Engineer Mode → Storage → Clear data). Because we never receive it, we cannot access, recover or delete it for you. (If you use Play Games cloud save, a copy is also kept by Google; see section 3.)

3. Third-party services and SDKs

  • No advertising: no ad network or ad SDK is included, and there are no in-app purchases.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional, later versions): you can sign in to Google Play Games for leaderboards, achievements and, when available, cloud save of your progress. The game asks Google to sign you in only when you tap the sign-in button or approve Google’s prompt. If you sign in, your Play Games player profile, leaderboard scores, achievements and the saved copy of your game progress are stored by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive them. To remove them, use Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data.
  • Anonymous usage and crash reports (optional, later versions, off by default): the App asks once, and you can change your mind in Settings → Privacy & data. If you allow it, Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) receives screen views, gameplay events (for example run results, upgrades bought and missions skipped), crash reports and diagnostics, your device model and Android version, and a random app-instance identifier. It does not receive your name, email address, contacts or location. Firebase processes this data for us under Google’s Firebase privacy terms. If you do not allow it, nothing is collected or sent.
  • The App is also built with Google’s open-source AndroidX / Jetpack libraries, which run entirely on your device.

4. Permissions

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: used only for haptic feedback (collisions, power-ups, alerts). You can turn it off in the App’s settings.

Version 1.0 requests no other permissions. Later versions also use android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which are needed by the optional features in section 3 (and, from Google’s libraries, a wake-lock permission to finish sending a report). The App does not request the advertising-ID permission.

5. Children

The App contains no ads, no chat and no social features. It does not ask for names, email addresses or other personal information. The optional features in section 3 are off until you choose them. If you are a parent or guardian and want data from a child’s device removed, email us (section 8) and we will help, and you can also turn the features off in Settings → Privacy & data.

6. Your choices

You control what the App stores and shares: turn sound, music and vibration on or off in Settings; allow or stop anonymous usage and crash reports in Settings → Privacy & data; sign in to or leave Google Play Games whenever you like; and erase all saved data on your device at any time by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your install, email us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes how it handles data (for example by adding an internet feature), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
