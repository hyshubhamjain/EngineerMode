Engineer Mode

Privacy Policy • Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary: Engineer Mode works fully offline. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything the game remembers stays on your device, and we never receive it.

This policy applies to Engineer Mode: Jetpack Runner (the “App”), published by Vishudh Tech Solutions (“we”, “us”).

1. We collect no data

The App does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission, so it cannot send information off your device. We do not collect, receive, store on a server, share, sell or monetize any information about you. In particular we do not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or address.
  • Device or advertising identifiers (Advertising ID, IMEI, MAC or IP address).
  • Location data, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or behavioral tracking.

2. What stays on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following in its private app storage (a local Room database and a Preferences DataStore). We cannot see it, and it is never transmitted:

  • Progress: your CPU Core, Memory Chip and Knowledge Chip balances, upgrades, unlocked characters, jetpacks, worlds and cities, missions, score multiplier and achievements.
  • Run history: the score, distance, world, city, character, jetpack and cause of failure of your past runs, and your Daily Outage results. The Daily Outage challenge is chosen from your device’s clock, not from a server.
  • TechPedia: which concepts you have studied or answered.
  • Settings: sound, music and vibration switches, whether you finished the tutorial, and your chosen touch-control area.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave the device even through your Google account. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Engineer Mode → Storage → Clear data). Because we never receive it, we cannot access, recover or delete it for you.

3. Third-party services and SDKs

  • No advertising: no ad network or ad SDK is included.
  • No analytics or crash reporting: no tracking, telemetry or crash-reporting library is included.
  • No accounts: there is no sign-in, social login or in-app purchase.
  • The App is built with Google’s open-source AndroidX / Jetpack libraries, which run entirely on your device.

4. Permissions

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: used only for haptic feedback (collisions, power-ups, alerts). You can turn it off in the App’s settings.

The App requests no other permissions.

5. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads, links out of the App, chat or social features. There is therefore no child data to share or protect beyond what stays on the device as described above.

6. Your choices

You control everything the App stores: turn sound, music and vibration on or off in Settings, and erase all saved data at any time by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App ever changes how it handles data (for example by adding an internet feature), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
